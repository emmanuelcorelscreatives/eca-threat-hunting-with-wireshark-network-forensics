# Case analysis template

- **Hypothesis and scope:** Host/address verification; collection link and window; visible directions; packet and host-clock formats; measured offset or `unmeasured`.
- **Pivots:** Wireshark filters, request/response graph series and interval, and packet pairs checked behind apparent bursts.
- **Observations:** Cited decoded packet references, endpoints, question names/types, replies or missing replies, retry decisions, and separately selected request/answer strings with inclusion rules. Cite host records separately.
- **Inference and alternatives:** Does possible timing overlap strengthen the channel hypothesis? Compare approved checks, application activity, and retry explanations without claiming cause.
- **Unknowns and confidence:** Missing process attribution, clock alignment, unseen resolver path, content meaning or sensitivity; state why confidence is limited.
- **Next action:** Name one bounded endpoint or resolver record request, its host and time window, and what would strengthen or weaken the hypothesis. Do not infer exfiltration from graph bytes or visible characters.
