Reopen each original and check both frame numbers using packet details, not row position. Open a chunk and check an original-to-chunk reference. A display filter does not renumber the file open in Wireshark; split and merged files do.
5. Compare SYN-A frames 3–4 with SYN-B frames 1–2 as an *authored overlap question*. Are recorded time and packet bytes equal? Can you attribute either indistinguishable merged row uniquely to its original? Preserve both entries. Consider other explanations for similar traffic in real cases, including a different collection point, retransmission or ordinary traffic. Do not infer a measured clock offset, a real duplicate cause or maliciousness.
