# Intake worksheet

Complete from the supplied documents only. Keep the answer guide closed. Do not record a packet finding, filter, incident confidence, or presumed user/device attribution.

1. **Business context:** What work must continue? Record stated hours, scheduled job, expected services, and the role that could confirm each exception.
2. **Assets:** For each of the four designated asset IDs, record role, owner, expected relationships, and whether its address mapping is merely claimed or absent.
3. **Capture listings:** For each of `CAP-01` through `CAP-03`, record stated source, claimed window, observation point, disclosed limit, and status. Which bounds or paths remain unverified?
4. **Engagement:** What may you use now? What action or unclear boundary requires coordinator confirmation?
5. **Hypothesis — one neutral question:** Name the designated population, the stated case window, and a behavior to compare with documented roles.
6. **Alternative explanation:** Give one ordinary workflow or operational possibility, without declaring it true.
7. **Unresolved evidence:** State what is missing. In general terms, what later evidence could support the question, weaken it, or leave it unresolved? Do not invent observations.

Stop after the intake note. The next lesson, **Plan the Investigation and Preserve the Evidence**, establishes an evidence record before traffic is narrowed.
