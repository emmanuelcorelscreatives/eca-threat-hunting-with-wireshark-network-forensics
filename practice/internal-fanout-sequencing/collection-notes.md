The baseline fixture intentionally has only one short exchange and supplies no independent full-window coverage proof or baseline job-cycle history. Treat its one-peer count as a limited comparison, not proof today's four are abnormal.

The isolated synthetic observation model exposes all declared bidirectional flows outside the gap. All file/job timestamps are labelled UTC; no independent clock-offset measurement exists. Do not shift clocks to fit a job. Inventory mappings and job records are synthetic context, not packet-derived identity or proof a job ran. Distinct IP addresses are four modeled peers here, not a universal method for counting real machines. NAT, remapping, inaccessible paths or capture mid-session can invalidate that inference in another dataset.
