# Answer Guide

No packet-specific numerical key is valid until the fixture is generated and checked by actual execution. Grade recorded packet details only against the verified release fixture.

## Expected reasoning

1. **Evidence boundary:** File identity, time coverage, observed directions, and Conversations results must come from inspection. The client-facing capture point is scenario metadata, not a packet-derived acquisition fact; record directions seen there without treating unseen directions or routes as absent. A `tcp` display filter changes the view, not the capture. Packet observations, fictional service context, inferences, and unknowns must be separated.
2. **Account question:** No account is established by a network conversation. Request identity evidence unless other available evidence and the risk of delay justify an authorized action.
3. **Port question:** Port 443 identifies an observed port value only. It does not prove TLS, a user, process, maliciousness, compromise, or transferred information.
4. **Service question:** `SVC-01` may share the peer, so its owner must assess impact and whether a narrower path control would work. The catalog note is not packet evidence.

## Decision-record checks

- **Host isolation** is bounded to an endpoint, may disrupt its functions, needs asset-owner impact assessment, incident-lead authorization, and endpoint-team implementation.
- **Account limits** remain conditional without identity corroboration. They must identify dependencies, preservation needs, approval, and identity-administrator scope.
- **Network blocks** must name the path and omitted routes, assess shared-service harm, and identify network-team implementation and service-owner review.

Every row should test an approved updater, service fault, routine transfer, or another plausible harmless explanation without asserting it occurred. It must include confidence limits, preservation ownership, disruption, cost of waiting, review time, and a change trigger.

The analyst recommends; the designated authority approves; the responsible team implements. Accept immediate action, conditional action, or urgent evidence collection when the rationale matches the evidence and delay risk. No control always comes first.

The ledger must keep original and copy separate, use only actual hashes and handling details, and leave the unavailable retention deadline open. Reject invented attribution, approvals, findings, exfiltration, or claims that containment is verified.
