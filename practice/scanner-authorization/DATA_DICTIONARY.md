# Synthetic case dictionary and evidence boundaries

This is separate, labelled Academy coursework, not the unavailable narrated-case capture. The four CSVs are **simulated operational records**, not packet-derived facts or owner testimony. Identifiers are local to this case; addresses are documentation-only. CSV times use ISO 8601 UTC with `Z`. Inventory `valid_from_utc` is inclusive and `valid_to_utc` exclusive. Semicolon-separated ID lists denote sets. A populated role, approval state, job status, or ticket does not confirm a host process, actual operator, service condition, packet attribution, or authorization of out-of-scope traffic; those confirmations are absent.

- `asset-inventory.csv`: `asset_id` unique key; `ipv4` documentation address; `role` simulated function; `owner` simulated managing team; `valid_from_utc`, `valid_to_utc` mapping validity. An endpoint or scanner role does not establish initiating direction or permission.
- `approved-tasks.csv`: `task_id` unique key; `source_asset_id` inventory key; `owner`; `planned_start_utc`, `planned_end_utc`; `display_time_zone` (`UTC`); `permitted_target_ids` inventory keys; `permitted_tcp_ports` decimal ports; `approval_state` (`approved`). A planned window is not execution or packet attribution.
- `scan-job-executions.csv`: `job_id` unique key; `task_id` approval key; `source_asset_id` inventory key; `actual_start_utc`, `actual_end_utc`; `configured_target_ids` inventory keys; `configured_tcp_ports`; `run_status` (`recorded_complete`); `record_owner`. Even this simulated execution row neither proves which process emitted a frame nor expands the approved target/port list.
- `task-tickets.csv`: `ticket_id` unique key; `task_type` (`administrator_maintenance` or `service_fault`); `source_asset_id` inventory key; `responsible_owner`; `expected_target_ids` inventory keys; `expected_tcp_ports`; `window_start_utc`, `window_end_utc`; `record_status` (`recorded_unconfirmed`). Neither ticket is confirmed owner or service testimony.

**Actual fixture specification:** `executed-fixture-spec.json` is the packaged, deterministic input to `offline-builder.py --spec … --output …`. Its top-level values include `version=1`, `synthetic=true`, `fixtureId`, `purpose`, `scope`, `observationPoint`, and an ordered `events` array. The 15 actual events have UTC `timestamp`, `kind=tcp`, documentation-address `src`/`dst`, integer `sport`/`dport`, TCP `flags`, 32-bit `seq`/`ack`, and empty `payloadText`. The authored flag codes are `S` SYN, `SA` SYN/ACK, `A` ACK, and `RA` reset/ACK. The second SYN to B and the second SYN to F repeat their respective source/destination addresses, ports, raw sequence, and empty payload. Their decoded frames and raw fields appear in `all-frames.stdout.txt`; a newer separate actual flags-field export is included as `decoded-fields.stdout.csv` with its exact execution receipt. The original all-frames field selection remains unchanged. Do not confuse raw `tcp.seq_raw` with potentially relative `tcp.seq` displays, or fixture event positions with cited frame numbers.

The builder emits a single-link Ethernet **PCAP**, not pcapng. `capture-info.stdout.txt` identifies 15 frames, microsecond precision, 1,074 **file** bytes, 810 **captured frame** bytes and a logical file interface; `all-frames.stdout.txt` gives UTC frame times and `tcp.len=0` for every frame. `protocol-hierarchy.stdout.txt` counts only Ethernet/IPv4/TCP. No DNS, UDP, ICMP, HTTP, application payload, file transfer, vulnerability check, or process attribution is supplied by this fixture. The builder may be capable of encoding other event kinds, but that does **not** put those protocols into this packaged capture or satisfy any case-pack requirement for them. Conversation byte counts include captured Ethernet frames, not TCP content.

`frame.time_utc` in the executed log is a human-readable UTC string such as `Jan 16, 2025 10:05:00.100000000 UTC`; `frame.time_epoch` is seconds since the Unix epoch with a fractional part. Neither is `frame.time_iso8601`: that field is unsupported by the pinned 4.4 tool inventory. `capture-info.stdout.txt` displays its earliest/latest times without a zone suffix; cite the explicitly UTC frame field or the fixture timestamps for UTC bounds.

**Names and missing context:** Earlier `fixture.json`/`fixtureSpec` and `offline_builder.py` names were draft proposals, not additional packaged copies of the actual JSON and hyphenated builder. Earlier proposed `data/discovery-comparison.pcapng`, `scripts/build_case.py`, `scripts/verify_case.py`, and `data/capture-context.json` are still absent. The spec's `observationPoint` describes one **authored** synthetic Ethernet link; it cannot verify a physical collection point, labelled interface, route, or visibility of traffic on other paths. A PCAP logical interface is not pcapng interface metadata. Implement and check that planned context and any mandatory missing protocol/data exercises separately rather than inventing their results. Bounded native Linux GUI checks are documented in `native-validation.md`; real owner/process/service confirmation and versioned publication have not occurred.
