## Defensible conclusion and validation

**Packet pattern:** strong *within-file* evidence of three distinct scanner-labelled-source targets in roughly three seconds, including one repeated SYN tuple, a reset from A, a TCP handshake at B, and a reset from C; separate administrator-labelled two-target contacts and single-peer retries are also visible. This is scan-like contact, **not proof of a vulnerability scan or adversarial intent**. Empty application payload and unknown physical/path coverage limit further claims.

**Authorization:** APP-01 plus the simulated JOB-01 provide a plausible, recorded in-scope explanation for the A/B:443 portion, **not a verified packet-to-job/process link**. They do **not** cover the observed C:445 contact. The full scanner-source activity therefore cannot be called a verified approved match; its reason and authorization remain **unresolved/inconclusive**, not automatically malicious. The administrator and fault tickets are plausible but unconfirmed alternative explanations for their **own** sources.
