# Scope map fields
Each row groups observed initiator, target, transport and destination port. A retry belongs in its existing row. source_file_id is the SHA256 of source-original.pcap (identical to practice.pcap); capture_start_utc and capture_end_utc are the declared scenario bounds, not measured sensor uptime. first_attempt_utc and last_attempt_utc are actual cited attempt packet timestamps. attempt_frames and reply_frames use semicolon-separated 1-based frame numbers. observed_initiator_ip and observed_target_ip describe direction checked in packet sequences, not device ownership. transport and destination_port describe packet headers, not a proven application. response_label states only visible sequence: complete initial handshake, reset replying to SYN, partial SYNACK, UDP payload, gateway ICMP quotation, or no reply captured. responder_ip is the actual outer response source; the ICMP gateway is not the quoted target. coverage_note is mandatory on every row and must name that row's observation limits. address_mapping_status and authorization_status remain unknown. interpretation_note separates hypotheses from observations. Count distinct targets separately from rows, retries, ports and packets. An unseen segment is unobserved, not quiet.
