# Answer Guide

This is a draft reasoning key for labelled examples, not executed evidence or case findings. Recompute the proposed arithmetic with the Academy checker before publication.

## A. Narration hypotheticals

1. Subtract the recorded three-hour offset: **15:20 − 03:00 = 12:20 UTC on October 7, 2026**. Retain the original wording and `+03:00`. As written, the source records minute-level precision; do not infer unrecorded seconds.

2. With no correction, 12:20:04 is four seconds after the illustrative exact time 12:20:00. Conditionally, **12:20:04 − 5 seconds = 12:19:59 UTC**, one second before it. The illustrative order therefore flips and depends on the measurement. A real minute-precision log would not independently establish second-level order.

A defensible correction identifies the device, independent time reference, comparison time, measured difference, uncertainty, and relevance to the event period.

3. No. The clock may have changed or drifted, and a later measurement may not support one adjustment across the capture.

4. `Z` identifies the display’s UTC basis. Fractional digits indicate timestamp resolution. Neither proves clock accuracy. UTC display changes presentation only; **Time Shift** is separate, can affect packets beyond the display filter, and an adjusted saved capture can contain changed timestamps.

## B. Supplemental synthetic exercises

**N1:** Fast by 5 ±2 seconds means subtracting between 3 and 7 seconds. The corrected interval is **12:19:57 through 12:20:01 UTC**. It overlaps 12:20:00, so order is inconclusive.

**U1:** UTC is **unresolved**. Identify the producing system, inspect its timestamp settings, and check whether the record names a zone elsewhere. Do not assign a zone because a difference appears to fit.

**U2:** A recorded numeric offset or another reliable disambiguating field is required before selecting one UTC instant.

## C. Packet observation

There is no fixed frame answer. Leave the fields blank until a validated synthetic fixture exists. A checkable response must pair the actual capture identity with its transcribed `frame.number`, `frame.time_utc`, and display format without claiming that the timestamp proves clock accuracy.

A final conclusion must say **supported**, **correction-dependent**, or **inconclusive**, with the reason.
