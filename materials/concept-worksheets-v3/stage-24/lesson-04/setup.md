# Setup

Source anchor: **After the threat hunt**

Use `setup.md`, `worksheet.md`, and `answer-guide.md` together.

Choose one input:
- your prior-stage capstone hypothesis and evidence register; or
- the explicitly synthetic fallback in the worksheet.

If prior work is unavailable, use the fallback and blank register. Never reconstruct missing references or results. No PCAP is supplied or required. Do not use private production data or credentials.

For an actual registered capture, work only in an isolated, authorized environment. In Wireshark, use **File, then Open**. Confirm source identity, collection window, capture point, and a colleague-locatable packet reference. Display filters hide packets; they do not remove packets or reveal uncollected traffic.

Optional only: the verified repository `https://github.com/emmanuelcorelscreatives/eca-threat-hunting-with-wireshark-network-forensics` contains `practice/dns-baseline/README.md` and its six-packet synthetic DNS fixture. It is not case evidence and cannot establish real compromise or a later connection.
