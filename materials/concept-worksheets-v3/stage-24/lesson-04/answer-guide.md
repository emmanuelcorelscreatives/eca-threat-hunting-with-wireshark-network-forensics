# Answer Guide

No predetermined capstone result is correct. Assess support, not suspicion.

## Review-ready rubric (10 points)

- **2:** Every material observation has source identity, reusable reference, time, capture point, and coverage; unavailable items are marked.
- **2:** Packet observations, external context, and working inference are separate; each claim has its own status and limit.
- **4:** One point each for attribution, encryption, missing traffic, and compromise when the answer includes a plausible alternative and smallest distinguishing request.
- **2:** A responsible owner, proportionate action, timing, operational/preservation effect, and success observation are stated.

**Required gates:** All material claims are traceable or unresolved; all four challenges are answered; no field is invented; the recommendation names an owner and decision criterion. Review-ready work meets every gate and scores at least 8/10.

## Reasoning key

- An address supports communication by an observed endpoint at the capture point and time. It does not identify a process, account, person, or intent. Asset mapping needs a separate record.
- Encrypted traffic may expose timing, endpoints, direction, or handshake fields, but absent names, certificates, requests, and content remain unavailable. Byte counts do not prove sensitive-data loss.
- No matching packet is only absence within demonstrated sensor coverage. Location, route, window, direction, truncation, and drops limit negative claims.
- Suspicion can justify investigation without proving execution, misuse, compromise, or loss. Endpoint, identity, approved-work, proxy, storage, or other authorized records may strengthen or weaken it.

## Synthetic exercise: checkable response

The strongest defensible result is `inconclusive`: the note reports communication, but its original source and reference are unavailable, so another analyst cannot repeat it. It supports no process, person, content, compromise, or environment-wide quiet claim. Approved synchronization remains plausible. A minimal first request is the identified source plus its window, location, and reusable reference; follow with only the endpoint, identity/work, or content-identifying record needed for the disputed claim. A defensible recommendation asks the responsible response owner to preserve relevant evidence and decide on prompt scoped investigation while recording operational impact. Success means traceability and corroboration raise or lower a specific claim's confidence.
