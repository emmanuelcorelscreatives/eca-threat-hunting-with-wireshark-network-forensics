# Answer guide — reasoning key

These are checks for reasoning, not captured findings or packet-count targets.

1. **Collection:** With no capture, leave the observation log blank. With an authorized capture, identify its name, collection point, window, missing direction, and visibility limits before attributing a sender. A short window or lost packets can hide relevant exchanges.
2. **Questions and replies:** A visible question establishes its source, destination, name, and type at the observation point—not application intent. A matched reply establishes that DNS information came back. Check DNS ID alongside endpoints, question, and conversation context; row adjacency is not a match. No visible reply does not prove none occurred. Clearing `dns` restores other packet context.
3. **Synthetic A:** If the stated hypothetical were supported by a capture, a bounded claim would be: ‘Contact recurred during the observed window.’ **Observed within the hypothetical:** recurring questions, matched replies, and the name’s appearance. **Inferred:** ordinary lookup, retry, health check, or a possible channel; appearance alone selects none. **Unknown:** purpose, command content or use, the data’s origin, and sensitive exposure.
4. **Test the stronger claims:** A command-exchange assessment needs meaningful instruction content and evidence about resulting behavior; an endpoint process record could help. A data-transfer or exposure assessment needs readable relevant content and an independent link to a file or application; carrying information in DNS alone is insufficient. Service-owner or application context can test an ordinary explanation, but a plausible story is not evidence that it fits.
5. **Synthetic B:** Beyond a recursive resolver, outward questions may identify the resolver, not the originating workstation; a suitable resolver record could address attribution. Missing incoming collection cannot establish that no reply occurred; capture coverage would need checking. Without authorized access to encrypted DNS contents, that connection does not reveal hidden names or answers. Request the smallest source addressing the specific unresolved question.

An answer is complete when it keeps hypothetical facts separate from the blank evidence log, states a limited claim, fairly tests an ordinary explanation, and leaves unsupported commands and exposure **Unknown**.
