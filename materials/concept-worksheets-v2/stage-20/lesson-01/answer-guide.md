# Answer guide

Full credit requires no invented evidence values or factual source/event rows.

1. **No-file state:** Source identity, observation point, coverage, displayed times, and file/interface details remain blank; coverage is `unknown` or `unavailable`, and continuity is `unverified`. Draw no solid coverage bars.
2. **Clock result:** Correction is `unavailable`; common time remains blank and ordering is `unresolved`. Separate per-file relative times are not a shared clock.
3. **Mapping result:** Asset identity is `unresolved`. A present-day inventory or displayed hostname cannot prove historical ownership. A public address cannot identify an internal device without a matching translation record.
4. **Window result:** Lower and upper bounds remain blank. No possible activity is placed or linked. The planned delivery, recurring outbound traffic, internal access, and a possible transfer remain unassigned to times or frames.
5. **Acceptable requests:** authorized working copies with original identifiers; collection notes documenting observation points and interruptions; an independently identifiable cross-source event or trustworthy clock record; historical assignment records covering the relevant time; and time-matched translation records where applicable. Requests identify needs without claiming the records exist. For this no-file exercise, leave each proposed request’s recipient blank with recipient status `unknown`, and mark request status `draft/not sent`; do not claim submission.
   - Authorized working copies with original identifiers: Which original file does each working copy trace, and what first/last packet times and file/interface details are available?
   - Collection notes: Which observation point, interruptions, and collection limits, if any, are documented for each source?
   - Independently identifiable cross-source event or trustworthy clock record: What clock relationship, correction amount, and uncertainty, if any, does it support?
   - Historical assignment records: Which asset, if any, held a numeric address at a candidate event time?
   - Time-matched translation records, where applicable: Which internal address, if any, corresponds to a public address at a candidate event time?
6. **Preservation reasoning:** Originals remain separate evidence. Distinct labels prevent a working copy from being mistaken for its source. Separate lanes prevent one sensor’s coverage from being extended into another’s; a missing packet is not proof that activity did not occur.
7. **Synthetic answers:** A—No; proximity does not measure an offset. B—No; the hostname is only a lead. C—Ordering is unresolved because the uncertainty is larger than the separation.
8. **Stopping check:** It cannot pass with the supplied material. The defensible response names the missing source, collection, clock, assignment, or translation record and requests it rather than inventing a packet, correction, hostname, or incident sequence.
