An observed start requires SYN = 1, ACK = 0, timestamp, source IP/port and scoped peer IP/port, with tuple/raw-sequence retry review. A completed observed handshake additionally requires its reverse SYN/ACK and following ACK. An indicator names a peer or domain; a durable candidate asks about behaviour that remains meaningful when the peer changes. Recurrence warrants review, not a maliciousness or command-channel verdict.
