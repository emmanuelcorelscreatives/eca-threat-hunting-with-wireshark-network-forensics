# Handoff worksheet

1. Record the detection question, existing proposal version, source, capture window, observation point and filter or pivot. Do not rewrite the existing analytic. For optional practice, inspect practice.pcap in Wireshark and enter exact frame references. Otherwise leave packet_observation null. Keep observations separate from inference, alternatives and confidence limits. A display filter cannot restore uncollected packets; DNS and encrypted traffic cannot identify a process or transferred content by themselves.
2. Inventory required target-feed fields, population, paths, timestamp usability, collection health and coverage limits. Packet fields do not prove the same fields exist in production telemetry. Ask the telemetry owner for evidence. The short synthetic window cannot prove later recurrence or visibility on internal paths.
3. In learner-handoff.json assign distinct detection and match-review roles, telemetry responsibility when another team owns the source, backup, change approver, missed-observation investigator and source-failure recipient. Record acceptance dates and evidence. In this cross-team exercise the template includes a telemetry role. Authorship is not acceptance.
4. Record the approved positive material, expected match and review destination before testing. After an authorized check record actual result and source reference, or retain unrun. Diagnose absent fields, analytic mismatch and delivery failures separately. Compare similar approved scheduled activity, document retain/exclude and harmless matches, and avoid a false-positive rate without a measured denominator. Confirm the reviewer can find the source event and proposed action.
5. Retain proposed status until accepted roles, verified source coverage, positive result and delivery each have evidence. Record proposal version, approver and a reversible response to excessive matches or collection failure. Ready-for-approval is not deployed.
6. In reviews name cadence, review owner and agreed next_date. For every early trigger (source change, missed case, excessive harmless matches, changed assets or paths), name proposal_owner and trigger_route: who sends what to whom through which channel, within what agreed interval, and how receipt is recorded. After review, record notification_ref, analytic/feed findings, proposal_owner_decision (retain/revise/pause), proposal_owner_decision_ref, reason and following_date. The match-review owner and proposal owner may differ. A trigger without the return route and documented owner decision is incomplete.
7. Rank G1 feed health/delivery, G2 observation-window coverage and G3 process identity by the decisions they block. Each gap needs its own owner, access/scope/effort considerations, acceptance check and evidence before closure. One improved feed does not close all gaps.
8. Run python3 verify-handoff.py learner-handoff.json. The untouched template should validate as proposed. Try a disposable copy with ready-for-approval while gates remain unrun; it must fail. Syntax validation cannot establish real evidence or authorize a test.

## Separate authorized-file assignment

With the data steward's authorization, use a working copy of a relevant capture or existing event record. Record provenance, window, observation point, permissions and collection limitations. Inspect only the approved scope, cite actual observations, and create a separate handoff. If no authorized file is available, record that dependency and keep findings unknown. Supply no sensitive records in a public repository. This assignment has not been performed by the course author on your behalf.


## Required handoff detail

Assess whether the responsible owner can inspect source health, coordinate authorized tests, read alerts and approve changes. Record each capability, access limitation and evidence reference in owners.capability_assessment; unknown access remains open even after a role is accepted. Give the reviewer a brief explaining the matched behavior, similar approved activity, evidence that could change the conclusion and an escalation contact; fill monitoring.reviewer_brief without treating a match as compromise.

Explain reviews.timing_rationale using the actual risk and the team's monitoring capacity. Discuss each gap's coverage gain, retention risk, authorized access and effort with responsible teams; in gap_priority_acceptance record who accepted the ordered priorities, when and the acceptance reference. Numeric ranking alone is insufficient.

reviews.decision and reviews.proposal_owner_decision represent the same final documented owner decision and must agree when either indicates completion. Even a proposed handoff needs a named review owner, proposal owner and route before recording a completed review. A proposal may remain proposed after a completed review because operational gates remain unmet. The validator checks basic routing and decision consistency; humans must assess capability evidence, reviewer content, cadence rationale and priority acceptance truth.
