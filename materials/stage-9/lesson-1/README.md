# Follow a Complete DNS Transaction

This kit accompanies Stage 9 Lesson 1 of the Academy Wireshark course. Begin with [setup.md](setup.md), read [collection-notes.md](collection-notes.md), complete [worksheet.md](worksheet.md) and [capture-exercise.md](capture-exercise.md), then compare [answer-guide.md](answer-guide.md). The unchanged header-only [dns-transaction-worksheet.csv](dns-transaction-worksheet.csv) has 33 columns for your own observations.

Two distinct samples are supplied: `practice.pcap` is a reproducible synthetic client/upstream model; `academy-loopback-dns.pcap` is an additional actual Academy-owned local collection with its own [collection notes](academy-collection-notes.md). Keep their hashes, pairs and attribution limits separate. Neither is the unavailable historical incident or a learner's completed own-capture assignment. [native-validation.md](native-validation.md) describes exact checked native views, CLI results and provenance.
