# Synthetic record schema

All dates are ISO-8601 UTC (`Z`). Assignment and approved-work intervals are **half-open**: start inclusive, end exclusive. Blank CSV fields mean **not recorded**, never that an event did not happen. These authored records are scenario metadata, not extracted packets or production logs. `ab:4` means frame 4 of the separately supplied and verified `ab.pcap`.

**`capture_inventory.csv`** — `capture_id`: `ab` or `bc`; `relative_file`: supplied separate PCAP; `simulated_sensor_location`: modeled placement; `window_start_utc`, `window_end_utc`: modeled collection bounds; `time_basis`: authored timestamp basis; `covered_link`, `excluded_link`: visibility model, not a physical measurement; `capture_filter`: modeled acquisition filter; `modeled_collection_gaps`: declared gaps; `clock_offset_status`: whether an offset was measured. `none` means none *modeled*, not independently verified.

**`address_assignments.csv`** — `asset_label`: A/B/C; `ipv4`: documentation-range address; `valid_from_utc`, `valid_to_utc`: assignment interval; `assignment_type`: illustrative assignment classification; `record_source`: synthetic inventory, not actual DHCP evidence.

**`host_events.csv`** — `event_id`: unique H-B-*; `host`: reporting asset; `ts_utc`: reported event time; `event_type`: `remote_session`, `scheduled_job_start`, or `process_network`; `account`: reported account; `process`: illustrative process label; `pid`, `parent_pid`: illustrative process identifiers; `job_id`: scheduled-job correlation key; `src_ip`, `src_port`, `dst_ip`, `dst_port`: reported endpoints, when recorded; `detail`: source-record context. A process-network row reports a connect request, not packet success.

**`identity_and_work_orders.json`** — `synthetic`: provenance flag. In `identity_events`, `event_id` is unique I-*; `ts_utc` is reported time; `source_system` names the fictional reporting system; `source_ip`, `target_host`, `account`, `service_label` describe reported access; `outcome` permits `success`; `session_ref` joins the illustrative access/session record. In `work_orders`, `work_id` is M-42; `valid_from_utc`, `valid_to_utc` bound approval; `source_asset`, `target_asset`, `transport`, `destination_port`, `account`, `job_id` define its specific scope; `status` permits `approved`. Approved work supplies context, not packet or causal proof. These arrays are additionally supplied as separate `identity_events.csv` and `work_orders.csv` downloads.

## Delivered implementation
The previously proposed files now exist: ab.pcap and bc.pcap are separate padded classic-PCAPs, identity_events.csv and work_orders.csv are exact string-field projections of the source JSON arrays, build_fixture.py constructs fresh sensor copies using offline-builder.py, and validate_fixture.py independently checks raw packets and scenario records. Older descriptions of pending captures describe the construction plan, not the delivered status. Neither capture contains a sensor clock measurement or genuine authentication protocol.
