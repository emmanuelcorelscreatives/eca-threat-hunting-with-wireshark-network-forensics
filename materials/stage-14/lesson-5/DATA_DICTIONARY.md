# Data and provenance dictionary

supplemental-boundaries.pcapng is the unchanged successful real Linux loopback capture. supplemental-boundaries.keys contains only its disposable owned TLS/QUIC session secrets. fixture-manifest.json binds hashes and measured packet totals. application-execution.json records real application-side negotiated ALPN and headers; it is not packet output. verification-report.json is actual TShark-derived before/after output and assertions, with every decoded frame and expert message. TCP/UDP stream indices are local to this capture, not global identities. Frame numbers are original capture numbers. frame.len is packet length; sum is captured packet bytes, not PCAPNG file size. IP is loopback in both directions; ports determine direction. Empty fields mean no decoded field at that layer, not proof no traffic existed.

HTTP/2 stream 0 is connection control; 1 and 3 carry requests. QUIC request streams 0 and 4 carry HTTP/3 exchanges; other visible stream IDs may carry control/QPACK. A packet can contain several frames/streams, so comma-separated field columns do not establish one-to-one arbitrary pairings. DATA/end-stream and stream FIN are completion evidence for the observed exchange; they do not prove no activity elsewhere.

native-capture-ledger.json binds six actual Linux Wireshark 4.4.0 screenshots to this capture. Their role is packet-list filter/result evidence, not hidden expanded details or a complete UI settings walkthrough. Native role reviews are retained separately by the production system and their exact hashes are delivered in native-independent-approvals.json. The final complete content review is outside the immutable kit. cli-execution.json binds actual commands/output. checker-rejection-proof.json records executed wrong-capture, wrong-key, wrong-total and absent-input rejection tests in temporary scratch copies.

The collection source is not a deterministic rebuild. New collection creates new keys/timestamps/packet scheduling and must not reuse this manifest or approval. Versions and local host provenance are retained. No source lecture, instructor identity, project ID or schedule was changed.
