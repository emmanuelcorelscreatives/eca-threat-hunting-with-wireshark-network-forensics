# Evidence classes and fields

practice.pcap: actual tcpdump capture, Ethernet loopback, immutable original. rpc-service-synthetic.pcapng: editcap conversion, original packet bytes/time/order preserved. “Synthetic” describes the deliberately authored activity and inert responder; packets were actually exchanged and captured. collection.json is the actual collection receipt. collect_rpc_lab.py is optional source requiring Impacket0.13.0/tcpdump and a new directory; it binds SMB and RPC listeners only to127.0.0.1 and uses ephemeral share storage.

expected-results.json: actual measured timestamps/IDs and TShark4.4.0 version. rows mirrors decoded-fields.csv; frame.number is the immutable original reference, frame.time_epoch is UTC Unix seconds, frame.time_relative is relative seconds, frame.len is captured packet length, tcp ports distinguish roles, smb2.sesid/tid/fid bind session/tree/file, dcerpc.cn_call_id and opnum are interpreted within their association/interface. Missing cells mean no decoded field, not absence in the environment. Exact command records are in execution.json; verifier output has its own execution receipt.

host-event-comparison-synthetic.csv: two authored hypothetical Windows event-shaped rows, not actual Windows events or exports. provenance must remain AUTHORED_HYPOTHETICAL. event_ref H01/H02 is a scenario reference; original_event_record_id is illustrative and carries no real audit provenance. computer/channel/provider/event_id/time_created_utc/service_name/image_path/process_id/parent_image/clock_context support identity and clock reasoning. Neither its2025 timestamps nor LAB-TARGET host are related to the actual2026 loopback capture. Do not merge their timelines. A hypothetical missing matching image is a limitation, not evidence of the real service's absence.

rpc-service-evidence-chain.csv is the learner blank; model-evidence-chain.csv is a completed bounded example. network_source/hash/frames/times, original_event_refs, hypothetical_refs, computer, clock_basis, service/image, authorization_ref, claim/limit/alternative/follow_up are separate. request_ref/reply_ref are original network frame numbers; a blank reply is missing in this capture. Hypothetical references never populate original_event_refs.
