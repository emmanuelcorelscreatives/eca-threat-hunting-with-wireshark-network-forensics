# Evidence entry rules

worksheet.csv is deliberately blank. One row can link a request to its response. Use original frame numbers and explicit UTC display times; tcp.stream and message_id are conversation context, not globally unique identities. status comes from a response. session_id is decoded linkage, never a human identity. tree_id only names a share when a corresponding visible TREE_CONNECT establishes that relationship in the relevant context. path is the requested name; FileId links an established open in session/tree context. create_action comes from a successful CREATE response. read_returned_bytes comes from the response data, not request length. write_accepted_count comes from the WRITE response. close_outcome requires its response. A missing response is unknown, not failure or success. Record observed absence only within a stated collection window; an empty cell means not filled, not observed absent. Use visibility_limit for encrypted, undecoded or missing context and explain why unresolved. Ports distinguish directions in this loopback file. Checksum offload artifacts and unsigned packets do not by themselves establish a compromise.
