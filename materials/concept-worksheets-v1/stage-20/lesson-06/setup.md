## Files and prerequisites

- `setup.md`: instructions and field key.
- `worksheet.md`: editable blank evidence and narrative register.
- `answer-guide.md`: reasoning key and acceptance checks.
- Tool: a UTF-8 Markdown editor. No credentials or production data are required.

No Project Seven packets, capture metadata, address assignments, endpoint records, or business records are supplied. The worksheet is intentionally blank; never substitute private or production data. The optional verified `practice/dns-baseline/README.md` synthetic DNS supplement is not required and cannot establish Project Seven compromise or links.

## Use

1. Enter `not supplied` whenever evidence is absent; never estimate a value.
2. Treat delivery, recurring outbound contact, internal contact, and possible transfer as hypotheses.
3. Preserve each available source's observation point, coverage, original time, measured offset, frame, analysis pivot, and address-mapping basis.
4. Compare the completed worksheet with the answer guide. No command or successful execution is claimed.

## Field key

- **Observation:** What an available packet directly shows.
- **Citation:** Original source, frame, time, and filter or conversation pivot.
- **Interpretation:** Strongest bounded meaning supported by cited sources.
- **Limitation:** Missing coverage, identity, visibility, content, or authorization.
- **Status:** `unresolved` without support; `observed` only with cited evidence; `excluded` only when supplied records justify removal from the suspected sequence.
