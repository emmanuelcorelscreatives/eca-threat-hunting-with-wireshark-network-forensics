# Answer Guide

This key assesses reasoning only. It does not supply packet findings, frame numbers, or numeric totals.

## Synthetic Exercise Answers

1. **Not yet meaningful.** Working traffic and overnight traffic are different operating periods. Match working mornings with working mornings under the same role and collection scope. Otherwise narrow the claim, obtain comparable coverage, or document the limitation.

2. **Do not compare the current totals.** Match the endpoint tab, exact display filter, and **Limit to display filter** state. IP rows represent addresses; UDP rows also include ports. Record whether each total is captured or displayed.

3. Write **'backup possible, unverified,'** rather than calling the traffic benign. Timing establishes only a lead. Check an execution record, the asset's role, the expected peer or destination, ownership, and authorization. Keep the packet observation, operational record, and linking inference separate.

4. Capture-relative first bins can align visually while representing different times of day. Use a comparable time-of-day view or document the shared-period alignment. Because the baseline omits the relevant period, the comparison is inconclusive. Obtain comparable coverage; do not treat the visible quiet period as evidence about uncollected time.

## Checkable Worksheet Standard

A defensible worksheet:

- matches role, collection point, scope, weekday, and operating period;
- records time zone, clock uncertainty, and missing coverage;
- matches filters, limit state, endpoint or conversation type, and start-time display;
- matches graph interval, filter, measure, and axis, without reading an average as a total;
- states what packets show without assigning cause;
- treats a remaining difference as a lead, not a verdict; and
- identifies the specific record or capture needed to resolve uncertainty.

With no supplied case evidence, the expected case result is **no finding**. Evidence fields and frame references remain blank rather than being invented.
