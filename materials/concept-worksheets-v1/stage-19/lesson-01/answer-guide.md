# Answer Guide — Reasoning Key

Use wording equivalents only when they preserve the evidence limits.

## 1. Expected assessment

| Dimension | Expected status and reasoning |
|---|---|
| Asset and contact | **Unresolved**; A reports an allegation, while B and C provide neither capture coverage nor asset mapping. |
| Destination or peer | **Unresolved**; A names only a service category, and D does not establish an observed peer or its owner. |
| Channel and visibility | **Unresolved**; B and E supply no packets or supporting records showing protocol, exchange type, or readable content. |
| Data sensitivity | **Unresolved**; C describes possible access, not classification or transfer of particular data. |
| Authorization | **Not established**; D is general context, while E lacks asset-, dataset-, purpose-, and window-specific approval. Missing approval evidence is not proof of unauthorized activity. |

## 2. Classifications

1. **Documented** — this describes Card A, not verified network contact.
2. **Unresolved** — no mapping links the records.
3. **Unresolved** — neither content nor transfer is shown.
4. **Not established** — a missing record does not prove prohibition.
5. **Interpreted** — further review is reasonable but is not a case fact.

## 3. Acceptable scoped question

For the asset and period referenced by the alert, can authorized records establish the asset identity, particular external peer contact, channel and content visibility, relevant data sensitivity, and asset-, dataset-, purpose-, and window-specific authorization?

This asks what must be established without treating the alert as proof.

## 4. Next record

A strong first request is the alert's authorized supporting network record with provenance and collection coverage. It addresses whether contact was observable before asset mapping, ownership, sensitivity, or approval is inferred. Another record is acceptable only if it is specific, authorized, and tied to the stated largest uncertainty.

## 5. Example statement

Card A documents that an alert alleged contact with an external service. One interpretation is that the allegation warrants scoped review. Without capture coverage, asset mapping, content, classification, and specific approval records, transfer, sensitivity, authorization, and data loss are not established.

A correct response cites only Cards A–E, separates observation from interpretation, states limitations, and assigns no volume, directionality finding, intent, or verdict.
