# Orientation reasoning key and reviewer check

1. The routine-explanation question is a **generic hypothesis**, not a finding. For this exercise, record the authorized source, capture name, observation point, collection window, time zone, supporting records, gaps, and readability as **unknown**. None is supplied. An unknown gap does not mean every path was captured; encrypted content is not assumed readable.
2. An actual filter or pivot and its reason belong under **Independent attempt and views**. Evidence belongs in the ledger. A frame number identifies a packet **within its source file**; the pair lets a reviewer find it. Packet time needs a stated time zone for comparison. Observed fields and what the packet visibly shows are not the analyst’s explanation of why it happened.
3. The view and ledger stay empty. There is no packet-backed conclusion or case confidence to rate. Do not infer a person’s intent, program execution, or an outcome from absent evidence. An inconclusive answer is appropriate when available case evidence cannot decide.
4. A useful future step is to inspect the **course-supplied authorized case brief and capture scope** before choosing a view. This could establish what source, observation point, collection window, time zone, and gaps an answer must account for. If required case material is missing, the Academy must supply it; the learner need not provide private data.
5. Attempt independently; use hints one at a time if needed; use the third to test against another explanation; open a supplied walkthrough **after** the attempt. This orientation has no case walkthrough to open.

**Reviewer check:** For orientation, mark packet source/frame, repeated view, and packet observations **not applicable**, not passed by fabricated entries. Pass the exercise when unknowns, an empty ledger, the non-finding label, hint order, and a consequential next step are present; otherwise mark **needs revision**. In a later case, require locatable source/frame, repeatable view, separated observations and interpretations, an alternative, confidence limits, and a specific next step. A supported inconclusive answer can pass.
