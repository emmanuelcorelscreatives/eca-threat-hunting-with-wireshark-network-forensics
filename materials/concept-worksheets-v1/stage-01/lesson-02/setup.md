## Shared evidence boundaries

- Separate visible observation from inference; test alternatives before deciding.
- Record capture source, observation point, covered time, and missing time.

> Packets can show conversations you observed. They may not identify the user prove execution, or reveal what's inside an encrypted transfer.
>
> No visible packet doesn't prove nothing happened.

- Label confidence. Request host, identity, or other evidence when packets cannot resolve the question.
