# Recovery and Improvement Worksheet

> **Synthetic exercise—supplemental, not an incident record.** No Project 7 capture or response result is supplied.
>
> A network-traffic capture goes quiet; a team may say “It’s gone.” What disappeared?

Use `not supplied` instead of inventing source identifiers, paths, times, peers, packet references, findings, or outputs.

## Record fields

Under every prompt, answer in this order:

`scope (source, point/path, window, interface/collection and filter limits) | bounded observation | marked inference and alternatives | evidence request (source, asset, time range) | owner role | status | completion criterion | recovery condition`

Allowed status: `open` or `closed—criterion evidenced`. An absent drop count does not prove complete collection. A display filter can hide loaded packets; a capture filter may have excluded traffic before recording. Replace each blank with as many lines as needed.
