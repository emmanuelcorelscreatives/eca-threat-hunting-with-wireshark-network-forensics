# Answer Guide: Reasoning Key

No fixed incident findings exist. Assess structure and bounded reasoning; `not supplied` is correct where evidence is absent.

- **SYN-01:** Bind the statement to its source, point/path, actual window, and collection/view limits. If supported: `No matching packets were observed at [point] during [window].` Other paths, missed periods, encrypted content, and host condition remain open; do not write `eradicated`.
- **SYN-02:** `session observed` is supportable only with the required references. Host control is a separate inference. Approved software and scheduled activity remain alternatives. Request targeted endpoint history or authorization evidence; the response lead assigns an appropriate owner.
- **SYN-03:** Keep root cause unestablished. Select evidence for the question it can answer: endpoint process history, identity records, service-owner change or authorization information, or another network viewpoint. Completion means assigned evidence was reviewed and remaining alternatives documented—not merely that a convenient repair occurred.
- **SYN-04:** This is a host-integrity conclusion, not a network-absence conclusion. The system owner records what was checked, what was found, and what remains unresolved. Quiet traffic cannot fill those fields.
- **SYN-05:** The service owner checks essential function and records the result and limits. The response lead owns any conditional restoration decision and accepted uncertainty. Restoration proves neither eradication nor completed analysis.
- **SYN-06:** `watch harder` is not a completion criterion. A valid improvement names the missing path or cause question, needed coverage or evidence, owner, and method for demonstrating coverage or narrowing alternatives. Until then, the original limitation remains open.

## Expected completion state

A complete worksheet has six unique IDs and no invented values. Network visibility, host condition, service restoration, cause, and improvement remain distinct. Every open question has an owner and targeted request; a closed status cites its criterion. Leave eradication unconfirmed until the evidence supports it.
