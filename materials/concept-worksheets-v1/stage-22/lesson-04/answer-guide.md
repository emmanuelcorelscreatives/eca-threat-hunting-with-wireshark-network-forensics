# Answer Guide and Reasoning Key

This lesson has no case-value answer. A correct submission preserves unresolved fields rather than manufacturing a convincing story.

| Area | Reasoning key and checkable result |
|---|---|
| Question and scope | Lead with what must be decided. Assets, intervals, observation points and visibility gaps are explicit. Unavailable values remain marked unavailable. |
| Sources | Original captures, working copies and supporting records remain separate. Because no Project 7 source is supplied, no identifier, hash, frame, timestamp, stream or filter is invented. |
| Inspection | Do not claim that a Project 7 filter was applied. A future entry must preserve the exact expression, explain its purpose and exclusions, and record any surrounding-traffic recheck. |
| Claims | Each sentence is labelled as an observation, inference or unknown. Confidence belongs to an individual claim and cites independent support, conflict or missing evidence. |
| Alternatives | Ordinary business activity remains a genuine competing explanation. Available material cannot support or weaken it, and no lead can be ruled out without verified context. |
| Systems | No system can be named as observed or potentially affected. Systems also cannot be cleared; source coverage and mappings are unavailable. |
| Limitations | Conditional impacts are accurate: a capture gap limits event order, missing direction limits conversation claims, encryption limits content claims, and an unverified mapping limits asset identity. Do not claim that a condition occurred. |
| Request | Name the unresolved claim, exact endpoint or identity record, relevant verified window and recipient if known. A broad request for more logs does not pass. |

**Synthetic writing example—not an incident or observed result:** `Source status: Unavailable—not supplied. Packet observation: none. Inference: no Project 7 conclusion can be drawn. Unknown: whether the proposed activity occurred.`

**Pass condition:** Every reviewer box is checked truthfully; unavailable case fields remain explicit; no compromise, user action, process action, account misuse, transfer content or data-loss conclusion is asserted.
