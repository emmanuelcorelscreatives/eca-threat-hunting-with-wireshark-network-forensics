# Answer guide — reasoning key

This key checks question framing, **not** packet counts or a finding.

1. In the synthetic roster, Laptop A and Laptop B fit a proposed laptop scope; Desktop C does not. Neither laptop is identified as the one in the note. No address or time-verified mapping is supplied, so neither can be matched to packets.

2. The source filename, first and last packet times, and coverage fields remain blank. A future capture’s observed bounds would constrain the window; its timestamps alone would not establish clock accuracy. A question may say **“during the capture’s verified window”** without supplying invented times.

3. The saved lesson’s model question is: “During the selected capture window, did the scoped workstations contact external peers not explained by documented business activity?” An acceptable exercise question uses the scoped *laptops* and a verified-window condition. It asks about observable contact and leaves room for an approved purpose; it asserts neither compromise nor a particular destination.

4. An answer would require an authorized capture, relevant coverage, verified asset/address mapping during its window, packet references, and an activity record specific enough to assess the contact. An update or work application is a possible explanation to **check**, not a documented explanation in this exercise. Visible contact alone cannot identify the initiating process or prove malicious intent.

5. The evidence template stays blank. With no capture, the status is **unresolved**, not “no matching traffic.” Relevant, attributable packets plus coverage and activity context could raise or lower concern. Even if a future file showed no matches, that would establish only absence in the selected file and window, subject to coverage.
