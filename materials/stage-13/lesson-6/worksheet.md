## Future learner work: your own authorized capture

Do not present this section as completed evidence. Obtain and record permission, approved asset and peer scope, retention and privacy requirements **before** opening an existing authorized capture or collecting traffic. For a permitted new collection, use **Capture → Options** to select the approved interface and check any capture filter and directions; start only within your approved window, stop, and save to a **new** file in an approved location. An existing authorized capture can instead be opened with **File → Open**. Preserve the original file; do not overwrite the supplied fixture. Record the authorization reference, capture point, interface, device and clock time zone, start and stop times, any interruptions or reported drops, capture/read/display filters, directions unavailable, actual first and last packets, file path and SHA-256, and the scoped asset, peer and peer port. Then perform the same GUI steps and fill this worksheet from *that file's* original frames. If no comparable events or reliable coverage exist, report that limit and do not invent a ranked finding.
**Menu-path scope:** menu paths in these instructions are for the learner to locate and verify on their installed version. The saved screenshots establish the windows and controls actually shown; they do not attest every intervening Statistics-menu click.
