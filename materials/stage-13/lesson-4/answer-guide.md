Stream2 has requests24 and28 at21 and31 seconds, responses26 and30. Thus recurrence to one peer includes three connections and two visible requests inside the third; connection count, packet count and visible request count are different. The quiet period between frame27 at21.3 and frame28 at31 does not establish application inactivity outside this designed trace or real sensor coverage. Graph intervals aggregate packets; the same pair of requests remains regardless of bin width.

The derivative's Expert output reports ACKed segment not captured and previous segments not captured; construction establishes the omission here, but those indications alone do not identify packet-loss cause in real traffic. The baseline has no intentional truncation. The executed baseline-retransmission.stdout.txt and limited-retransmission.stdout.txt are empty for tcp.analysis.retransmission in TShark4.2.2. This is a bounded dissector result, not proof that a real capture had perfect coverage. The derivative builder reassigns IPv4 identification and recomputes checksums after removing the event; check_capture.py normalizes only those fields for cross-file event matching.

A defensible final statement identifies the exact file, host/peer, filters, representative frames, three observed TCP connections, reused-stream activity and deliberate derivative limits.
