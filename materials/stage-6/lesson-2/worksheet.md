# Worksheet — observed transport symptoms

Use `synthetic-tcp-evidence.pcapng`, not the original PCAP. These are synthetic observation records; the task is to separate visible evidence from possible explanations.

1. State your hypothesis: repeated transmission, reordering, collection duplication, or unresolved? Record file hash, version, capture interval, available interface names and the absent real collection/receiver context.
2. Open Expert Information. Record whether the list is restricted by a display filter. Try `tcp.analysis.retransmission`, `tcp.analysis.lost_segment`, `tcp.analysis.duplicate_ack`, `tcp.analysis.out_of_order`, `tcp.analysis.fast_retransmission` and `tcp.flags.reset == 1` separately. Record actual original frame numbers or no matches; decoder labels can differ by version. A filter cannot reveal unrecorded traffic.
3. For a matching candidate, retain its source frame number and choose Conversation Filter → TCP. Inspect both directions and neighboring packets, including those hidden by the diagnostic filter. Compare raw first-byte sequence numbers and payload lengths as half-open byte ranges. Record reverse ACKs (the next byte expected) and timestamps before and after the candidate. Does a later ACK advance? This does not prove application completion or the sender's recovery algorithm.
4. Compare PCAPNG frames 4 and 5: interface IDs, timestamps, direction, payload bytes, sequence range, ACK and flags. Contrast frame 11. Does the supplied construction provenance establish a cloned observation? Would equivalent fields in an unknown capture alone prove duplication? What other collection evidence would you request?
5. Investigate the observed-order gap at frame 13 and the lower range at frame 15. Use reverse ACKs to test explanations. Distinguish a gap in recorded packets from proven network loss; missing directions, late recording and capture loss remain possible in a real trace.
6. Identify the reset sender and its surrounding traffic. Clear the reset filter, then clear all filters and inspect the entire window. Can the trace establish the reset's cause, receiver acceptance, application result or malicious intent? Return to Expert Information with the conversation scoped and check its filter limitation again.
7. Submit a short evidence report: hypothesis; source file/hash/frame; capture scope and limits; filters; ranges, reverse ACKs, timing and reset direction; strongest inference and confidence; credible alternative; missing telemetry and next authorized action. Include honest no-match outcomes. The next lesson follows streams without losing this transport context.
