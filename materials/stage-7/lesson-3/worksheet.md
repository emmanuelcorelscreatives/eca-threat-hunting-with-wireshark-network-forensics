4. Apply a candidate conversation filter with the context menu and record the actual resulting expression. Confirm bidirectional packet coverage; compare with `tcp.stream == 0`. Inspect frames 6 and 7: record `frame.len`, `frame.cap_len`, `tcp.len`, relative and raw sequence values, and retransmission indication. Can you sum the two payload lengths as eight new bytes?
5. Inspect frame 32. Distinguish reported original frame size, captured size, reported TCP payload length and captured payload availability. Can its checksum or missing payload content be verified? Leave unique application data unknown where evidence is insufficient.
